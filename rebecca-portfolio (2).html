About me

The person behind
the systems

I'm Rebecca — Becky to my clients. I'm a Business Operations & Systems Specialist with over three years of experience working as the operational backbone for founders, executives, and growing teams across the UK, US, and beyond.

What makes me different is how I think. I don't just complete tasks — I look at your entire operation and ask: what should be automated, what should be documented, and what should never land on your desk again? Then I build the systems that make that happen.

I've worked across executive support, workflow automation, content operations, CRM management, and project coordination — always with the same goal: less chaos, more clarity, and a business that runs even when you're not watching it.

I'm based in Lagos, Nigeria, working remotely with clients worldwide. I pick up new tools fast, communicate proactively, and treat every founder's business with the same level of care I'd bring to my own.
